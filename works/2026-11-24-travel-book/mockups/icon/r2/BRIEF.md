# 「여행 한 권」 launcher icon — round 3 brief

Already shown, do not repeat: round 1 (6 drawn books), round 2 HTML (꺾쇠 brackets+dot on brick, fanned
pages on cobalt, "한 권" on green, numeral 1, shelf spines, "tb"), Codex icon_A..D (brackets+dot on brick,
four print textures = one design). Failure of last round: one shape idea, one colour field, only texture varied.
This round varies three axes at once: **shape idea × colour field × rendering style**.

Quality bar (what the good ones do): Polarsteps = one shape on one loud field. Day One = one object part
(bookmark), not the whole object. VSCO = pure geometry, no meaning needed. Things/Notion = soft light on one
solid form, light background. Bear/Duolingo = a face you remember. Arc = colour itself is the brand.
Common: one silhouette, 2–3 colours, reads at 48 px, survives a circle mask and a one-colour themed icon.

## 1. Debate

Candidates: B1 offset sun on a spread · B2 「권」 type mark · P1 thick book, striped page edge ·
P2 zigzag fold-out book · U1 book with eyes · U2 real photobook with a photo set into the cover.

**Round 1 — pitch and attack**

- Brand: B1 — a sun cut by the book's centre fold, right half slipped up like a page mid-turn. "A moment,
  bound." Abstract, ownable, no object clichés. B2 — the name's own quote marks around 권; the wordmark and
  the icon become one thing.
  Attacks: P1 is "a book", every reading app has one. U1 mascots age fast and a solo studio cannot feed a
  character. U2 is a stock photo, not a mark.
- Product: P1 — one solid form, light field, soft shadow; page edge in four colour bands says "photos became
  pages" with zero detail inside the silhouette. P2 — zigzag silhouette exists nowhere else in a launcher
  grid; four panels = four flat colour areas, trivially adaptive.
  Attacks: B1 at 48 px is two half circles = contrast/brightness toggle. B2 dies in monochrome theme if 권
  is thin, and English stores read nothing. U1's eyes vanish in themed icons. U2 photo detail turns to mud.
- User (25): U1 — "I tap things with a face. Toss, Duolingo, 당근 all have a creature or a wink." U2 — "a
  real object looks expensive, like an indie photobook shop, not an app template."
  Attacks: B1 looks like a bank or a weather app. B2 on black looks like a webtoon/novel reader. P1 "is
  this Notion?" P2 "is that a folded map? maps are the pin cliché in disguise."

**Round 2 — rebuttal and repair**

- Brand concedes B1 needs the spread visible: put the sun on a paper square with a real fold line so it is
  a book first, a sun second. Concedes B2 must use a heavy geometric 권, brackets fat enough to carry alone.
  Still rejects U2: "a book cover with a picture on it" is the same silhouette as P1, just noisier.
- Product concedes P1 is generic unless the striped page edge faces the viewer and takes a third of the
  form. Answers the map attack on P2: panels are flat colour-photo blocks with rounded light, no lines, no
  roads; standing upright, not lying open. Accepts U1 only if the silhouette alone (book standing on its
  open covers like legs) is the character — eyes are a bonus, not the mark.
- User accepts P2 once it stands up ("that's cute, like a tiny exhibition"). Drops U2: "fine, it's the
  same as P1 but worse small." Insists U1 stay: "five clever marks and no warm one is how you lose 20s."
  Asks B2 to move off pure black to warm ink so it is not a webtoon app.

**Director's call** — five survive, U2 is cut (same silhouette as P1; photo detail fails at 48 px).

## 2. Concepts

### C1 — 접힌 해 / Folded Sun
- Idea: a sun printed across a book spread; the right half has slipped upward, like a page caught mid-turn.
- Fits: says "a moment, bound into a book" with two shapes; zine/riso grammar; no travel-object cliché.
- Risk: at small size may read as a weather or contrast-toggle icon. Themed mono: works (two half discs).
- IMAGE PROMPT:
  > Square 1024×1024 mobile app icon artwork, flat vector graphic, full-bleed solid deep green background
  > (#0B6B4A) reaching every edge, no rounded-corner mask, no border. In the centre, a warm off-white paper
  > square (#F4EFE7) occupying about 56% of the canvas width, perfectly flat, with one thin vertical dark
  > line (#2B2621) down its exact middle like the centre fold of an open book. On the paper square, one
  > large solid brick-red circle (#C8401F), about 34% of canvas width, cut exactly in two along the fold
  > line: the left half sits lower, the right half is shifted straight upward by one quarter of the
  > circle's diameter, so the two half-discs are clearly offset. Crisp geometric edges, completely flat
  > colour, exactly three colours plus the dark fold line, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no grain.
  > Bold, minimal, modern Swiss poster style, generous empty green margin around the paper square.
  > Must NOT appear: any text, letters, numbers, rays, clouds, horizon lines, waves, mountains, extra
  > shapes, page curls, 3D effects, device frames, watermarks.

### C2 — 사진 단면 / Photo Edge
- Idea: one chunky closed book seen from the page-edge side; the page block is banded in colour — the
  trip's photos, seen edge-on.
- Fits: the product in one object (photos → one volume); light field stands out in a grid of loud icons;
  Things/Notion-level calm polish.
- Risk: without the bands it is a generic book; the bands must be big. Themed mono: silhouette of a slab.
- IMAGE PROMPT:
  > Square 1024×1024 mobile app icon artwork, clean stylised 3D render, full-bleed warm off-white
  > background (#F4EFE7) reaching every edge, no rounded-corner mask, no border. One single thick closed
  > hardcover book, centred, filling about 58% of the canvas, shown in a three-quarter view from slightly
  > above so we see the front cover and, prominently facing the viewer, the long page-edge side. The cover
  > is plain matte cobalt blue (#2A45AE), completely blank, with softly rounded corners. The book is very
  > thick, almost a block: the visible page-edge side is as tall as one third of the cover's width. The
  > page edge is divided into four wide, clean horizontal colour bands stacked from top to bottom: brick
  > red (#C8401F), cream (#F4EFE7 slightly darker for contrast, #E6DDCF), deep green (#0B6B4A), cream
  > again (#E6DDCF). Soft diffuse studio light from the upper left, one gentle soft contact shadow under
  > the book in warm grey, smooth matte material, simplified geometry like a premium productivity app
  > icon, no fine page lines. Calm, minimal, lots of empty background.
  > Must NOT appear: any text, letters, title, logo or pattern on the cover, bookmark ribbon, second book,
  > photos, camera, table, wood grain, props, reflections, glossy plastic look, watermarks.

### C3 — 꺾쇠 한 글자 / Bracket Glyph
- Idea: exactly three glyphs — 「 권 」 — "one volume", quoted the way Korean titles are.
- Fits: the wordmark 「여행 한 권」 and the icon share one device; type-led like a real indie publisher's
  imprint; dark field is the only dark icon in the set.
- Risk: non-Korean stores read nothing; image models may misdraw 권 (final asset will be redrawn as
  vector anyway — the render only has to sell the look). Themed mono: works, it is already one-colour type.
- IMAGE PROMPT:
  > Square 1024×1024 mobile app icon artwork, flat typographic logo, full-bleed solid warm near-black
  > background (#2B2621) reaching every edge, no rounded-corner mask, no border. Centred composition of
  > exactly three glyphs within the central 60% of the canvas: the Korean corner bracket 「 at the upper
  > left, the single Korean Hangul syllable 권 in the centre, and the Korean corner bracket 」 at the lower
  > right. The syllable 권 (consonant ㄱ, vowel ㅝ, final consonant ㄴ) is drawn very large and very bold
  > in a geometric modern Korean sans-serif (like a heavy Gothic display weight, even stroke width, square
  > terminals), coloured warm off-white (#F4EFE7), about 36% of the canvas tall. The two brackets are thick
  > solid right-angle L-shaped strokes with the same stroke weight as the letter, coloured brick red
  > (#C8401F), hugging the letter diagonally: 「 just above-left of it, 」 just below-right of it.
  > Completely flat colour, crisp vector edges, no gradients, no shadows, no texture.
  > Must NOT appear: any other text, Latin letters, numbers, extra Hangul characters, dots, circles,
  > underlines, serif or brush or calligraphy lettering, decorative shapes, 3D effects, watermarks.

### C4 — 병풍책 / Fold-out
- Idea: a small accordion (concertina) photo book standing up in a zigzag; each panel is one colour-washed
  "photo".
- Fits: a trip unfolding page by page; the zigzag silhouette is unique in any launcher grid; real paper
  under real light gives the "indie photobook shop" feel no flat icon has.
- Risk: could be read as a folded map or brochure — panels must be pure colour, no lines. Themed mono:
  zigzag outline works well.
- IMAGE PROMPT:
  > Square 1024×1024 mobile app icon artwork, photorealistic studio product photograph, full-bleed seamless
  > solid cobalt blue backdrop (#2A45AE) filling every edge, no rounded-corner mask, no border. One single
  > small accordion-fold (concertina, leporello) paper book standing upright on the blue surface, unfolded
  > into a clear zigzag of exactly four tall rectangular panels, viewed from the front and slightly above,
  > centred and filling about 60% of the canvas width. The paper is thick matte card. The four panels,
  > left to right, are each one plain solid printed colour with a thin off-white paper margin around it:
  > brick red (#C8401F), warm off-white (#F4EFE7), deep green (#0B6B4A), warm off-white (#F4EFE7). The
  > folds are sharp and alternate in and out so light and shade alternate from panel to panel. Hard, warm,
  > single-source sunlight from the upper left casting one crisp zigzag shadow onto the blue surface to
  > the lower right. Shallow, clean, modern editorial still-life, very minimal, nothing else in frame.
  > Must NOT appear: any text, letters, numbers, map lines, roads, printed photographs of real places,
  > people, hands, other objects, pens, cameras, plants, wood, table edges, extra books, logos, watermarks.

### C5 — 권이 / Little Volume
- Idea: a tiny book standing on its half-open covers like two legs, two dot eyes on the front — the app's
  character.
- Fits: the one warm, tappable icon in the set; 20s users remember faces (Duolingo, Bear); gives the solo
  studio a mascot for empty states, share cards and reels later.
- Risk: can look like a kids'/education app; needs restraint (no mouth, no arms). Themed mono: eyes drop
  out, the standing-book silhouette must carry it.
- IMAGE PROMPT:
  > Square 1024×1024 mobile app icon artwork, flat vector character illustration in a bold sticker style,
  > full-bleed solid brick-red background (#C8401F) reaching every edge, no rounded-corner mask, no border.
  > One single small cute book character, centred, filling about 55% of the canvas: a chunky closed-ish
  > hardcover book standing upright seen from the front, its two covers opened slightly at the bottom so
  > it stands on them like two short stubby legs, forming a soft upside-down V at the base. The book body
  > is warm off-white (#F4EFE7) with generously rounded corners and a thicker cobalt blue (#2A45AE) spine
  > strip running down its left side. On the front cover, slightly above centre, exactly two small solid
  > round dot eyes in warm near-black (#2B2621), set fairly close together, giving a calm, slightly
  > curious look. The whole character is tilted about 6 degrees to the right as if taking a step.
  > Thick simple shapes, flat colours only, one small flat darker-red oval shadow under the feet. Modern,
  > friendly, minimal, like a top-chart consumer app mascot.
  > Must NOT appear: any text, letters, mouth, eyebrows, arms, hands, blush cheeks, sparkles, stars,
  > hearts, speech bubbles, glasses, hats, second character, outlines in black, 3D clay or plastic
  > rendering, gradients, background patterns, watermarks.

## 3. Render rules (Codex)

1. Render each concept **twice** from the prompt exactly as written (second pass = a fresh generation of the
   same prompt, not an edited prompt). Size 1024×1024, PNG, opaque background.
2. Save into this folder (`…\scratchpad\icon-gen2\`): `c1_a.png c1_b.png c2_a.png c2_b.png c3_a.png c3_b.png
   c4_a.png c4_b.png c5_a.png c5_b.png`.
3. Look at every render before keeping it. **Reject and regenerate** (max 3 tries per file) if any of:
   - stray text, letters, numbers or a watermark (C3: anything other than exactly 「 권 」, or a malformed 권);
   - an extra object, a second book/character, props, or decorative clutter;
   - a rounded-square "icon tile" drawn inside the canvas, a border, or a device/phone mockup;
   - key shape touching the outer 20% of the canvas (must survive a circle mask);
   - background not one continuous field of the specified colour;
   - C1 not flat (shadows/gradients), C2 cover not blank, C4 panels showing lines/photos, C5 with a mouth or arms.
4. If a file still fails after 3 tries, keep the best one and name what is wrong in a one-line `NOTES.txt`.
5. Do not add textures, grain or "print" variants — one clean render per slot. Do not composite, crop or
   retouch. Do not touch the repo.
