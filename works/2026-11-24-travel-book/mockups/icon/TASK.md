# Task: generate launcher icon artwork with your image generation tool

Use your built-in image generation tool (not code, not SVG, not PIL drawing). Save results as PNG files in this folder.

## The app
Android app named "여행 한 권" (Latin name: tripbook): pick trip dates, the app makes a flip-through photo book of the trip. Users: Korean, 20-30s. Look of the app: warm paper #F4EFE7, ink #2B2621, accents cobalt #2A45AE, brick red #C8401F.

## Chosen concept: "꺾쇠" (corner brackets)
A bold brick-red (#C8401F) square field. On it, two paper-white Korean book-title corner brackets 「 and 」 (top-left and bottom-right), like a camera viewfinder's corner marks framing the centre, and one small round ink-dark (#2B2621) dot in the exact centre. Flat, graphic, modern indie-zine/photobook feel. No text, no letters other than the two bracket shapes, no camera body, no lens rings, no gradients that look like 3D glossy app icons, no drop shadows, no border.

Reference of the vector draft the operator liked: see draft.png in this folder (match its composition, make it feel crafted rather than computer-drawn: slightly printed/risograph or paper-cut quality, subtle paper grain allowed on the white strokes, crisp at small size).

## Make
1. icon_A.png .. icon_D.png: 4 variations, each 1024x1024, full-bleed square (Android adaptive icon foreground+background flattened; keep the brackets and dot inside the central 61% so a circle mask does not cut them):
   - A: closest to the draft, crisp flat print.
   - B: paper-cut look (white brackets as thick cut paper with a hair of shadow on the red), still flat overall.
   - C: risograph look (slight grain, slightly misregistered ink dot), still very legible.
   - D: your best alternative within the same concept (e.g. dot replaced by a tiny ink book silhouette, or brackets with rounded ends).
2. Look at each result yourself. Regenerate any that has text, extra objects, a lens/camera, broken symmetry, or is unreadable when imagined at 48 px.
3. Write NOTES.md: one line per file describing it.

Do not touch anything outside this folder. Do not commit. Finish by listing the files you saved.
