Rules extracted: (a) one object or one word, never both fighting; (b) one dominant colour field + at most
two accents; (c) strokes and letters heavy enough to survive 48 px — no hairlines; (d) a word works only
if it is ≤ 4 glyphs per line or ultra-condensed heavy caps; (e) depth comes from soft shading on the object,
not floor shadows or textures; (f) recognisable *thing* beats geometry.

## 2. Why rounds 1 and 2 failed

1. **No subject.** Round 1 (A–D) is two corner brackets and a dot — a camera-focus/scan UI glyph. Nothing
   says book, photo or travel; four "options" were one idea with rounded corners.
2. **Geometry instead of a thing.** r2 c1 (split circle on a spread) and r2 c4 (folded colour panels) are
   abstract studies; the viewer has to be told what they mean. c4 reads as a flag/folding screen.
3. **Object without the idea.** r2 c2 is a well-rendered chunky book — but only "book" (dictionary, reader
   app). No photo, no trip. Striped page edges read as a flag. Also floats small on an empty field.
4. **Illegible type.** r2 c3 「권」 built from blocks is hard to read even for Koreans, means "volume" only
   to someone who already knows the name, and reuses the failed bracket motif.
5. **Childish, unfinished character.** r2 c5 is a blob with two dot eyes: no travel attribute, wobbly
   edges, clip-art flatness. Across both rounds: flat fills, no light, no material, palette used as
   decoration rather than as one owned colour field.

## 3. Concepts

Shared canvas rule for every prompt: 1024×1024, full-bleed flat background colour to all four edges (no
rounded-corner tile, no device frame), all key shapes inside the central 60 % (≈ 205 px margin each side),
one subject only.

Shared MUST NOT list (append to every prompt): no rounded-square icon tile or border, no phone or mockup,
no extra objects, no props on the ground, no map pins, no globe, no airplane, no camera, no compass, no
people, no hands, no faces or eyes unless stated, no extra text, no small print, no watermark, no
signature, no stray marks or speckles, no paper grain noise, no gradient mesh background, no floor line,
no long cast shadow, no clip-art outline style, no stock-vector look, nothing cropped by the canvas edge.

### C1 — Instant Cover  (direction 1: instant photo × book)  ★ strongest
- **Idea:** A closed hardcover book whose front cover *is* an instant photo: square picture window, thin
  margins on three sides, the deep blank "chin" at the bottom. Cloth spine on the left, page edges on the right.
- **Why 48 px:** cream rectangle + coloured square high in it = instant photo, instantly; the spine strip
  and page-edge lines turn it into a book. Three flat fields, no detail needed. Monochrome layer: rounded
  rectangle, solid spine bar, square cut-out with a dot.
- **Risk:** reads as "just a polaroid" if the spine is too thin — spine must be ≥ 14 % of book width.

**IMAGE PROMPT**
> Square 1024×1024 app icon artwork, full-bleed solid cobalt blue background #2A45AE. Centered, a single
> closed hardcover book seen from the front, portrait format about 4:5, rotated 6 degrees clockwise,
> occupying the central 60% of the canvas. The front cover is warm paper white #F4EFE7 and is laid out
> exactly like an instant photo print: one square picture window in the upper part with equal narrow
> margins at top, left and right, and a clearly deeper blank margin below it. Along the left edge of the
> book a brick red #C8401F cloth spine strip, about one seventh of the book's width, with a subtle hinge
> groove where it meets the cover. On the right and bottom edges, three thin stacked cream page-edge layers
> show the book's thickness. Inside the square window, a flat graphic sunset: apricot-to-brick sky
> (#F0A368 to #C8401F), a cream sun disc, one dark ink #2B2621 hill silhouette along the bottom. Matte
> paper and book-cloth materials, soft studio light from the upper left, a gentle highlight on the cover's
> top edge, a soft tight contact shadow in darker cobalt directly under the book. Premium, minimal,
> top-tier App Store icon quality, crisp clean edges. No text anywhere.
> Must NOT appear: [shared list], no lettering on the cover, no second photo, no bookmark ribbon.

### C2 — Flip Stack  (direction 1)
- **Idea:** A small thick book made of instant photos, seen three-quarter, front cover lifted ~25° as if
  mid-flip, showing the next photo-page beneath. The flip is the product ("flip-through photo book").
- **Why 48 px:** chunky white block with one warm square on top and a wedge of opening — an object with
  real mass and one moment of motion (like Journi's leaning spine). Monochrome: block outline + lifted cover line.
- **Risk:** the lifted cover is hard for an image model (warped pages, extra sheets); may drift toward a
  generic photo stack.

**IMAGE PROMPT**
> Square 1024×1024 app icon artwork, full-bleed solid warm paper background #F4EFE7. Centered, one small
> thick square-ish book viewed from a three-quarter angle slightly above, within the central 60% of the
> canvas. The book is bound along its left edge with a cobalt blue #2A45AE cloth spine. Every page is an
> instant photo print: white frame, square picture, deeper blank margin at the bottom. The front cover is
> lifted open by about 25 degrees, hinged on the spine, gently curved like stiff card, revealing the page
> underneath. The cover's picture is a flat graphic sunset in brick red #C8401F with a cream sun disc; the
> revealed page's picture is a flat deep green #0B6B4A hillside under a pale sky. The page block is about
> one quarter as thick as the book is wide, with fine even page layering in cream. Soft 3D render, matte
> materials, smooth studio light from upper left, soft ambient occlusion in the opening, small soft contact
> shadow. Premium, minimal, top-tier App Store icon quality. No text anywhere.
> Must NOT appear: [shared list], no loose photos, no more than one lifted page, no rings or spiral binding,
> no stripes on the page edges.

### C3 — Carry-on Book  (direction 2: a book that looks like a traveller)
- **Idea:** The book is packed for the trip: an upright hardcover with a leather carry handle on its top
  edge, one luggage strap around it and a hanging luggage tag — a book-suitcase. Character through posture
  (a slight forward lean), not through a face.
- **Why 48 px:** rectangle + handle arc is the universal suitcase silhouette; the cream page block on the
  fore-edge keeps it a book. One object, one colour field, soft 3D like 토스/당근. Monochrome: book
  outline, handle arc, strap band.
- **Risk:** can read as "suitcase" only, or as a luggage app. Page edges and spine must stay visible.
  No face by design — the dot-eyed blob of round 2 is what made it childish.

**IMAGE PROMPT**
> Square 1024×1024 app icon artwork, full-bleed solid warm apricot background #F2B98A. Centered, one
> hardcover book standing upright, seen from a slight three-quarter front angle so the front cover, the
> rounded spine on the left and the cream page block on the right fore-edge are all visible, leaning
> forward 7 degrees as if setting off, within the central 60% of the canvas. The cover is cobalt blue
> #2A45AE book cloth. A short tan leather carry handle #B5763A arcs from the top edge of the book like a
> suitcase handle, fixed with two small brass rivets. One tan leather luggage strap wraps vertically around
> the book with a small brass buckle on the front. A small cream luggage tag hangs from the handle on a
> short brick red #C8401F cord. Soft 3D render, matte cloth and smooth leather, gentle studio light from
> upper left with a soft highlight along the spine, small soft contact shadow under the book. Refined,
> premium, grown-up object design, top-tier App Store icon quality. No text anywhere, the tag is blank.
> Must NOT appear: [shared list], no face, no eyes, no arms or legs, no wheels, no stickers, no hat,
> no second strap, no writing on the tag or cover.

### C4 — trip/book  (direction 3: wordmark icon)
- **Idea:** Lowercase "trip" stacked on "book", 4 + 4 letters, left-aligned, heavy geometric sans. The
  stem of the "p" runs straight down and *becomes* the stem of the "k" — one shared vertical joins the two
  words like a binding. The dot of the "i" is a small tilted brick-red square: a photo.
- **Why 48 px:** four glyphs per line is the limit that still reads (29CM, ZARA, instax). Cream on one
  cobalt field. The shared p/k stem and the red square are the two ownable details. Monochrome: the
  wordmark as is, square dot included.
- **Risk:** image models misspell or mangle the shared stem — expect a high reject rate; final must be
  drawn in vector. Latin wordmark for a Korean-named app.

**IMAGE PROMPT**
> Square 1024×1024 app icon artwork, full-bleed solid cobalt blue background #2A45AE, completely flat.
> Centered within the central 60% of the canvas, a two-line lowercase wordmark in warm paper white #F4EFE7:
> first line "trip", second line "book", left-aligned, the two lines set tight. Typeface: bold geometric
> sans-serif in the manner of Futura Bold — perfectly circular bowls, single-storey shapes, flat terminals,
> tight tracking of about minus 2%. Exactly eight letters: t, r, i, p on the first line and b, o, o, k on
> the second. The vertical stem of the "p" continues straight down without a break and is also the vertical
> stem of the "k" directly beneath it, so the two letters share one continuous stroke. The dot of the "i"
> is not round: it is a small solid square in brick red #C8401F, rotated 8 degrees. Pure flat vector
> graphic, razor-sharp edges, even stroke weight, no effects.
> Exact text: "trip" / "book". Must NOT appear: [shared list], no other words or letters, no capital
> letters, no serif, no outline or shadow on the letters, no underline, no icon or symbol beside the text,
> no texture.

### C5 — Masthead  (direction 4: travel-magazine cover cropped into an icon)  ★ strongest
- **Idea:** The icon is the top of a magazine cover. "TRIPBOOK" as a one-line masthead in ultra-condensed
  heavy caps; below it the cover image, and — as on real covers — the subject rises *in front of* the
  masthead: a mountain peak overlapping the bottom of the letters, a brick sun behind them.
- **Why 48 px:** Condé Nast Traveller proves a 8–9 letter masthead reads if it is ultra-condensed, heavy,
  and one light colour on one blue. Below: one triangle and one disc. Word bar + peak + sun = "travel
  magazine" without reading. Monochrome: letters + peak outline, sun as a ring.
- **Risk:** the overlap can make letters ambiguous (keep overlap under 15 % of letter height, only on
  "P" and "B"); spelling errors in renders.

**IMAGE PROMPT**
> Square 1024×1024 app icon artwork designed as the cropped front cover of a premium travel magazine,
> full-bleed solid cobalt blue #2A45AE sky filling the whole canvas. In the upper part, a single-line
> magazine masthead reading "TRIPBOOK" in warm paper white #F4EFE7, all capitals, set in an
> ultra-condensed heavy grotesque sans-serif in the manner of Knockout or Druk Condensed Bold — tall narrow
> letters, uniform heavy strokes, tight tracking — spanning exactly the central 60% of the canvas width,
> letters about one quarter of the canvas tall. Exactly eight letters: T, R, I, P, B, O, O, K. Below and
> slightly overlapping it, one large flat-graphic mountain rising from the bottom of the central area: deep
> green #0B6B4A with a clean paper white snow cap, its peak just covering the bottom edge of the letters
> "P" and "B" so the mountain sits in front of the masthead. Behind the masthead on the right, one brick
> red #C8401F sun disc, partly hidden by the letters. Bold flat editorial illustration, crisp vector edges,
> a very subtle soft light gradient on the mountain face only. Top-tier App Store icon quality.
> Exact text: "TRIPBOOK". Must NOT appear: [shared list], no other words, no issue number, no date, no
> barcode, no cover lines, no price, no lowercase letters, no serif, no clouds, no trees, no second peak.

### C6 — Issue One  (direction 4)
- **Idea:** The magazine as an object: one perfect-bound issue floating at a slight angle, cream cover,
  masthead "TRIPBOOK" in a high-contrast Didone, and one square cover picture with a deep lower margin —
  the instant-photo proportion again, tying it to C1.
- **Why 48 px:** tilted cream slab with a dark word-bar on top and one blue block on a brick field: reads
  "a magazine" from shape and spine even when the word is too small to read. Monochrome: slab outline,
  bar for the masthead, square.
- **Risk:** Didone hairlines disappear at 48 px (see Artifact Uprising) — the word becomes texture, which
  is acceptable here but weaker than C5; 3D text often warps.

**IMAGE PROMPT**
> Square 1024×1024 app icon artwork, full-bleed solid brick red background #C8401F. Centered, a single
> perfect-bound magazine seen from the front at a slight three-quarter angle, rotated 8 degrees
> counter-clockwise, floating, within the central 60% of the canvas; its flat square spine is visible on
> the left and thin cream page edges on the bottom. The cover is warm paper white #F4EFE7. Across the top
> of the cover, a one-line masthead reading "TRIPBOOK" in dark ink #2B2621, all capitals, high-contrast
> Didone serif in the manner of Bodoni Bold Condensed — thick vertical stems, sharp flat serifs, tight
> tracking — spanning the full width of the cover inside its margins. Exactly eight letters: T, R, I, P,
> B, O, O, K. Below the masthead, one square picture with narrow side margins and a deeper blank margin
> beneath it: a flat graphic seascape in cobalt blue #2A45AE with a paper white sun disc on the horizon.
> Soft 3D render, matte coated paper, studio light from the upper left, a soft highlight along the spine
> edge, small soft contact shadow in darker brick. Premium editorial feel, top-tier App Store icon quality.
> Exact text: "TRIPBOOK". Must NOT appear: [shared list], no other words, no issue number, no barcode, no

## 4. Render rules (Codex)

1. Render each concept **2 times** from its prompt (replace `[shared list]` with the shared MUST NOT list
   from section 3). Output 1024×1024 PNG.
2. Save into this folder as `c1_a.png`, `c1_b.png`, … `c6_a.png`, `c6_b.png` (12 files). Do not write
   anywhere else; do not touch the repo.
3. **Reject and re-render** (up to 3 extra attempts per slot, then keep the best and note the defect) if any of:
   - text is misspelled, has extra/missing/merged letters, or any text appears where none was asked
     (C1–C3 must have zero text; C4 exactly `trip` / `book`; C5 and C6 exactly `TRIPBOOK`);
   - extra objects, a second subject, props, people, faces;
   - stray marks, speckles, texture noise, watermark, border or rounded-tile frame;
   - a key shape is cropped by the canvas edge or extends outside the central 60 %;
   - background is not one flat full-bleed colour of the specified hex family;
   - clip-art look: black outlines, cartoon shading, wobbly edges, stock-vector gloss.
4. Before accepting a render, downscale it to 48×48 and look at it: the subject must still be identifiable.
   If not, reject.
5. No Play Store 512 px versions, no adaptive-layer splits, no monochrome versions in this round.
6. Report per file: accepted on attempt N, or the remaining defect.
