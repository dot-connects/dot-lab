# Android StandBy competitors (research 2026-11-12, web search; ratings/installs as the sources showed them)

- Leader: Zetabit "StandBy Mode: Clock & Widgets" (br.com.zetabit.ios_standby) — 1M+, ~4.3 (≈30K), free + ads + premium.
- Others are small (≤100K): MAA "StandBy Mode Always On Display" 100K+ 4.0; Foldy (fold-focused) 3.5 (26 reviews),
  subscription $0.89/mo after a 3-day trial; DockScreen 3.1; Dock Clock OS 27 Standby; samvd "StandBy (Desk & Night
  Clock)" free/no ads; Focus Dock; several "StandBy … Always On Display" clones.
- Naming: 10 of 12 put "StandBy" in the title; Play appears to allow it (no policy doc or takedown found).
- Complaints found: hard/unstable setup; price ("not worth the $40", Foldy "$9"); wish for a one-time ad-free unlock;
  calendar widget shows all calendars (no filter); two screen-off options behave the same; must be unlocked to run;
  burn-in worry (articles); too many permissions. Not found: battery/heat, Samsung restrictions, alarm issues.
- Praise/requests: desk/bedside/photo frame use; start conditions (always, wireless only, Wi-Fi, screen off, schedule);
  night mode; burn-in protection; many clocks; one-time unlock; calendar filter; easier setup.
- Gaps for us: one-step onboarding (permissions inline, r10), cheap one-time unlock, calendar filter, burn-in defaults,
  stable foldable support (Foldy is weak), Samsung/other OEMs (Pixel has Hub Mode / screen saver).

Sources: play.google.com listings, appbrain.com, droid-life.com (2026-10-22), techradar.com, xda-developers.com,
beebom.com, makeuseof.com, androidauthority.com (standby-mode-on-android-phones-3558674, recreated pixel 10 feature).
